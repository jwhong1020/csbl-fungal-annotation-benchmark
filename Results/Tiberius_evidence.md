## Methods

RNA-seq reads were aligned to the genome using HISAT2. Transcript structures were assembled using StringTie, 
and RNA-seq alignments were converted into gene prediction hints.

Protein evidence was provided from the `Fungi.fa` sequence file. 
These protein sequences were aligned to the genome using Miniprot and converted into protein-derived hints.

RNA-seq and protein hints were integrated, and high-confidence gene models were selected and processed for Tiberius training. 
The final structural annotation was generated as `tiberius_train.gff3`.

Annotation statistics were calculated from the final GFF3 file. 
BUSCO completeness was evaluated in protein mode using the `fungi_odb12` lineage dataset.

## 5. Protein sequence and representative isoform analysis

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Representative proteins selected by the current script | 12,242 |
| Genes in final GFF3 | 11,281 |

The representative-protein count was greater than the gene count in the final GFF3 file. Therefore, the current longest-isoform selection result should not yet be interpreted as one representative protein per final gene.

This discrepancy indicates at least one of the following:

1. The protein FASTA identifiers were not grouped correctly by the current regular expression.
2. `tiberius_proteins.fa` may not correspond exactly to `tiberius_train.gff3`.
3. The protein FASTA may contain proteins from an intermediate Tiberius prediction rather than only the final trained annotation.

The protein FASTA headers and the transcript-to-gene relationships in `tiberius_train.gff3` should be checked before using `Tiberius.longest_isoform.faa` for a final tool-to-tool comparison.

## 7. Overall interpretation

Tiberius predicted 11,281 genes and 15,670 transcripts, with an average of 1.39 transcripts per gene. Most transcripts were multi-exonic, and no CDS models shorter than 150 bp were detected.

The protein BUSCO completeness was 87.3%, with 85.9% single-copy and 1.4% duplicated BUSCOs. Fragmented and missing BUSCOs accounted for 6.8% and 5.9%, respectively.

The structural annotation statistics were successfully calculated from `tiberius_train.gff3`. However, the protein FASTA and representative-isoform analysis require additional validation because the number of representative proteins was greater than the number of genes in the final GFF3.
